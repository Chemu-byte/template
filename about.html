History of Our Creation

Folks Gym was founded in 2014 in a converted matatu garage in Nairobi's Westlands. Brian Otieno, a former rugby conditioning coach, and Wanjiru Kamau, a physiotherapist, wanted a gym where a retired teacher, a new mother, and a university student could train side by side. The name comes from Brian's greeting to everyone who walked in: "Karibuni, folks!

With "pay what you can" memberships and a free Saturday Beginner Hour, it grew by word of mouth. Today Folks Gym has three branches in Nairobi, Kisumu, and Nakuru, serving about 2,500 members.
